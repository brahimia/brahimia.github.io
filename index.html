About

I'm a postdoctoral fellow at UNC Charlotte's Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, where I serve as project manager on an NSF Civic Innovation Challenge deployment of AI-enabled video analytics across ten pilot sites in Uptown Charlotte. My Ph.D. dissertation, Co-Creating Responsible AI for Public Safety, reflects the way I've always worked: policy design, statistical modeling, and human experience have to agree with each other before a system should be deployed.

My work spans federal grant writing, mixed-methods stakeholder research, supervised machine learning, and UX evaluation — often within the same project. I teach graduate and undergraduate courses in data science and machine learning, and mentor students across data analytics and UX research.
