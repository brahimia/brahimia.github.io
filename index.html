About

Get to know me

Postdoctoral Fellow & Responsible AI Researcher

I'm Babak, a Ph.D. in Public Policy from UNC Charlotte, working at the intersection of AI policy, machine learning, and human-centered design. My research asks how AI-enabled systems — especially in public safety — can be governed responsibly, built rigorously, and experienced fairly by the communities they touch.

I currently serve as a Postdoctoral Fellow in UNC Charlotte's Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, managing an NSF Civic Innovation Challenge deployment of AI-enabled video analytics across ten pilot sites in Uptown Charlotte. I teach graduate and undergraduate courses in data science and machine learning, and mentor students across data analytics and UX research.
